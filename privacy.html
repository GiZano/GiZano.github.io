Privacy Policy

giovanni-zanotti.is-a.dev — last updated: 22 November 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how the personal portfolio website giovanni-zanotti.is-a.dev ("this site") collects and uses information when you visit it. This site is a static portfolio hosted on GitHub Pages and does not run e-commerce, user registration, or database services.

2. Data Processing & Prior Consent

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, EU 2016/679) and the Italian Data Protection Authority guidelines (Garante Privacy Provv. 231/2021), statistical tracking is disabled by default.

On your initial visit, no tracking script is loaded, no remote analytics connection is initiated, and no analytics cookies are stored. Only if you explicitly grant permission via the cookie consent banner, Google Analytics 4 (GA4) is loaded to collect aggregate usage statistics, such as:

  • Anonymized aggregate browsing metrics (page views, session duration, device type, approximate geographic region);
  • Referrer domain and browser type (GA4 architecture does not log or store visitor IP addresses).

3. Cookies & Consent Management

This site uses no profiling or marketing cookies. Statistical cookies from GA4 are loaded strictly upon prior consent. If you decline, close, or ignore the consent banner, no analytics cookies are placed on your browser.

4. Local Storage on Your Device

The site stores two minimal, functional flags in your browser's localStorage:

  • gizano-site-lang: Remembers your language preference (Italian or English);
  • gizano_analytics_consent: Stores your cookie preference (granted or denied) so you are not prompted on every page load.

These values remain exclusively on your local device and are never transmitted to external servers.

5. Self-Hosted Infrastructure & Third Parties

To eliminate unauthorized data transmission to third-party content delivery networks:

  • Self-Hosted Typography & Frameworks: All font files (Inter, Exo 2, Rajdhani), icons (Font Awesome), and framework libraries (Bootstrap CSS and JavaScript) are hosted locally on this origin. No IP addresses or requests are sent to Google Fonts or jsDelivr CDNs.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC): Dispatched only upon explicit user opt-in. See Google's privacy policy.

6. Your Rights

Under the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, or request deletion of any personal data concerning you. Since this site operates as a static portfolio without user accounts, data processing is strictly limited to aggregate, anonymized statistics. To exercise your rights or submit inquiries, contact: contact@giovanni-zanotti.is-a.dev.

7. Changes to This Policy

Any updates to this policy will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
