1. Data Controller & Scope

This privacy policy explains how the personal portfolio website giovanni-zanotti.is-a.dev ("this site") handles information when you visit it. This site is a static portfolio hosted on GitHub Pages and does not operate e-commerce, user registration, comment systems, or server-side databases.

Data Controller: Giovanni Zanotti — Contact: contact@giovanni-zanotti.is-a.dev.

2. Legal Basis & Prior-Consent Architecture

In compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, Regulation EU 2016/679) and the Italian Data Protection Authority guidelines on cookies and tracking tools (Garante Privacy Provv. n. 231 del 10 giugno 2021), statistical tracking is completely disabled by default.

  • Statistical Analytics (GA4): Processed exclusively upon freely given, specific, informed, and unambiguous opt-in consent pursuant to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR. Prior to explicit consent, no tracking script is loaded, no remote network request is initiated, and no cookies are written.
  • Functional Storage (Local Storage): Technical parameters (language selection, cookie consent state) are processed under the legitimate interest of providing essential website functionality pursuant to Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR and Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive (Directive 2002/58/EC).

3. Google Analytics 4 & Data Retention

Only if you actively click "Accept" on the cookie consent banner, Google Analytics 4 (GA4, operated by Google LLC) is initialized asynchronously to collect high-level aggregate usage metrics, including:

  • Aggregated metrics (page views, session duration, device type, approximate geographic region at country/city level);
  • Referrer domain and browser family. GA4 architecture does not log or store individual IP addresses (IP addresses are dropped at the edge ingestion servers).

The following strict privacy protections are enforced in the analytics configuration:

  • Data Retention Window: User and event data retention is configured to 14 months (the maximum standard retention period before automatic and irreversible deletion from Google Analytics servers).
  • Google Signals Disabled: Google Signals cross-device tracking is disabled (allow_google_signals: false) to prevent user identification across Google accounts and services.
  • Ad Personalization Disabled: Advertising personalization and remarketing features are disabled (allow_ad_personalization_signals: false).

If you decline, close the banner (✕), or ignore the banner, no analytics cookies are stored and no external script is loaded.

5. Local Storage on Your Device

The site stores two minimal, functional flags in your browser's localStorage:

  • gizano-site-lang: Remembers your preferred display language (Italian or English);
  • gizano_analytics_consent: Stores your cookie preference (granted or denied) so you are not prompted repeatedly on every page load.

These values reside solely in your browser and are never transmitted to external servers.

6. Self-Hosted Infrastructure & Third Parties

To eliminate unauthorized data leakage to third-party CDNs:

  • Self-Hosted Typography & Frameworks: All font files (Inter, Exo 2, Rajdhani, Merriweather WOFF2), vector icons (Font Awesome), and framework scripts/styles (Bootstrap CSS and JavaScript) are hosted locally on this origin. No IP addresses or requests are sent to Google Fonts, Google Hosted Libraries, or jsDelivr CDNs.
  • Google Analytics 4 (Google LLC): Dispatched only upon affirmative user consent. For details, see Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Optional On-Demand Video Embedding (QuakeGuard Project): The QuakeGuard project page offers video demonstrations that can be activated on demand. Only when you actively click the preview button to watch the video, an embedded player from YouTube (operated via youtube-nocookie.com in privacy-enhanced mode) is loaded into the page. No network request or cookie is sent to YouTube prior to your explicit click.

7. Your Rights under GDPR

Under Articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR, you have the right to access, rectify, restrict, or request the deletion of any personal data concerning you, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing based on consent prior to its withdrawal. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Italy, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali).

To exercise your rights or submit inquiries regarding data protection on this site, contact the Data Controller directly: contact@giovanni-zanotti.is-a.dev.
