Privacy Policy

giovanni-zanotti.is-a.dev — last updated: 10 August 2026

1. Introduction

This privacy policy explains how the personal portfolio website giovanni-zanotti.is-a.dev ("this site") collects and uses information when you visit it. This site is a static portfolio hosted on GitHub Pages and does not run e-commerce, login, or data-submission services.

2. Data we collect

The only third-party service that processes aggregate usage data is Google Analytics 4 (GA4). When you visit this site, Google Analytics may set cookies and collect information such as:

  • Anonymized browsing statistics (pages visited, time on page, device type, approximate location);
  • Referrer and browser information.

The site itself is static: it stores no databases, user accounts, or personal forms.

3. Cookies

This site does not use essential cookies. GA4 may use cookies to distinguish unique visitors and track session statistics. These are analytics/performance cookies, not used for advertising or profiling. You can block or delete them through your browser settings, or use Google Analytics' official opt-out browser add-on.

4. Local storage on your device

To remember your preferred language (Italian/English), the site stores a small preference flag named gizano-site-lang in your browser's localStorage. This value never leaves your device and is used only to restore your language choice on the next visit.

5. Third-party services

  • Google Analytics — usage statistics. See Google's privacy policy.
  • Bootstrap, Font Awesome, Google Fonts — CSS/JS/font assets served via CDN (jsdelivr, fonts.googleapis.com). Your browser may contact these CDNs to load resources.

6. Your rights

Depending on your jurisdiction (e.g. GDPR in the EU), you may have the right to access, rectify, or delete personal data related to you. Since this site does not store personal accounts, the data processed is limited to aggregate analytics. To exercise your rights or ask questions about this policy, contact the site owner at gizano.dev@gmail.com.

7. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the "last updated" date at the top of this page.
