Privacy Policy for LevelUp

Effective Date: September 26, 2026

LevelUp is a local-first application. We believe your productivity and goals are your business, not ours.

Data Collection

The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on remote servers. All your tasks, peaks, and settings are saved locally on your device via SQLite and AsyncStorage.

Analytics & Tracking

We do not use third-party analytics, crashlytics, or any form of tracking software.

Permissions

LevelUp may require specific local permissions to function optimally:

  • Calendar Access: Used exclusively to synchronize your planner with your local Google Calendar (or other on-device calendars). We do not send your calendar events to any external servers. This synchronization happens locally on your device.
  • Notifications: Used for local reminders about your scheduled tasks and peaks.

Contact

For any questions, please open an issue on our GitHub Repository.
