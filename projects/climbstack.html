Engineering Case Study

ClimbStack: Conquering Decision Fatigue

An offline-first mobile productivity engine built with React Native and SQLite that gamifies long-term goals into scalable mountain peaks, powered by an intuitive weekly time-blocking system.

Visit Live Website Read Article on Hashnode Source Code Google Play

Case Study Overview

Productivity tools often fail because they create more administrative overhead than actual progress. ClimbStack was designed from first principles to bridge the gap between abstract annual resolutions and concrete daily hours. By coupling a gamified mountain-climbing metaphor with an offline-first SQLite persistence layer, ClimbStack eliminates daily micro-decisions and protects focus without cloud friction.

The Problem: Decision Fatigue & Overload

Traditional task systems suffer from two fundamental design flaws:

Endless To-Do Lists

Monolithic backlogs overwhelm the user, generating constant cognitive guilt rather than actionable momentum.

Rigid Calendars

Minute-by-minute calendar blocking collapses as soon as an unexpected real-world interruption occurs.

The result is decision fatigue: spending mental energy negotiating what to do next instead of actually executing. ClimbStack solves this by separating long-term vision from daily focus.

The Core Mechanic: Peaks & Base Camps

Turning abstract goals into a tangible visual journey:

In ClimbStack, high-level ambitions (such as earning a degree, launching a product, or mastering a language) are framed as Peaks to conquer. Each peak is segmented into incremental milestones called Base Camps.

As completed time blocks accumulate, a dynamically generated SVG mountain fills up progressively with color. This provides immediate visual satisfaction and reinforces habit persistence through intrinsic reward.

The Engine: Weekly Time-Blocking & Daily Tunnel Vision

Frictionless time architecture designed around real human routines:

Weekly Templates

Predefine structured recurring routines and duplicate entire weeks with a single tap.

Bespoke Categories

Tag activities with custom hex colors and icons, maintaining a balanced breakdown between study, fitness, coding, and rest.

Today's Focus Mode

Hides the entire week's schedule to present solely the current day's blocks, removing ambient anxiety and context switching.

Architecture: 100% Local-First & Zero Tracking

Engineered for speed, resilience, and absolute data sovereignty:

React Native & Turbo Modules

Native 60 FPS mobile UI performance across both Android and iOS devices.

Embedded SQLite Database

Zero-latency local transactions. The app works flawlessly anywhere — on airplanes, in rural commutes, or on actual mountain trails.

True Privacy by Design

Zero telemetry, no third-party trackers, and no mandatory login wall. Your data remains strictly on your device.

Complete JSON Data Export

One-tap backup and export of your entire local database, giving users full ownership and portability.
